About ShuddhVox

Created by Tarun

What it does

ShuddhVox (Shuddh means pure, Vox means voice) removes background noise from recordings. Use it on call recordings, vlogs, interviews or film audio. If you give it a video, it can also make a new video with the cleaned sound.

How it works

  • It learns the noise from the quietest parts of your file.
  • It then lowers that noise in every frequency, as much as the cleaning level allows.
  • In Call and Vlog modes the voice volume is also balanced.
  • Everything happens on your phone. Your file never goes to a server.
  • The app is free. Ads are shown while a file is being processed.

Limits

It works best on steady noise such as fan hiss, wind, air conditioners and constant crowd hum. Changing sounds, like another conversation or music in the background, are not fully separated. That needs an AI source-separation model, which can be a later upgrade.
